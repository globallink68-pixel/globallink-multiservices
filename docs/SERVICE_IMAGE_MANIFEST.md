# Service image manifest

All artwork is original AI-generated conceptual imagery. The hero was generated as a standalone scene. Most services were generated in category contact sheets, then cropped as separate assets and reviewed individually. PAN Services uses standalone artwork without government emblems. These scenes illustrate use cases and do not depict Global Link staff, premises or partners.

Images live in `frontend/src/artwork`. Vite emits hashed files into `dist/assets`. The homepage is 960 × 600 and service images are 720 × 450 WebP. Cards lazy-load images. The initial hero image loads eagerly. CMS hero uploads take precedence. A failed upload falls through to slug artwork, a valid CMS icon where available, then a local icon.
